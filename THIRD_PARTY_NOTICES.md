The initial recommended fallback model is **Qwen3-8B**, published by Qwen at <https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-8B>. Its model card identifies the repository as Apache-2.0 licensed. Obtain the model files directly from the model publisher and retain the licence and notices distributed with those files. Ara does not claim authorship of or ownership over Qwen or its weights.

Qwen’s official local-runtime guide documents the `Qwen/Qwen3-8B-GGUF` repository, the `qwen3-8b-q4_k_m.gguf` quantization filename, and llama.cpp support beginning with version `b5092`: <https://qwen.readthedocs.io/en/stable/run_locally/llama.cpp.html>. Runtime and hardware compatibility can change; consult that guide and the model repository when selecting files.
