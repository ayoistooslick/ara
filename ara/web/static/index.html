Beyond the dictionary

Meaning is rarely just the words.

Ara separates literal sense, intended meaning, region, register, and uncertainty. When a phrase is genuinely ambiguous, it asks instead of guessing.

Figurative

“I don chop breakfast this morning.”
Literal
I ate a morning meal.
Intended
I was dumped or defeated — usually said with a wry laugh.

Register: informal · Nationwide · Confidence 0.92

Ambiguous on purpose

“Na wa o.”
Literal
“It is something else.”
Possible
Exasperation at a price. Admiration of an outfit. Mild disbelief.

Ara asks for the cue it is missing. It does not invent one.

Regional, not universal

“Sannu da aiki.”
Language
Hausa, not Pidgin.
Meaning
Well done at work — a greeting of appreciation.

Ara names the source language instead of flattening everything into “Nigerian”.

One core, two interfaces

Built for the way the day actually goes.

01

Talk, type, or speak

Text in the terminal and browser. Voice input and spoken replies in the browser where the device supports them, with visible microphone state and an immediate stop.

02

Read the room

Standard English, Nigerian English, Pidgin, or another language — chosen from your register, not a stereotype. It will not force slang into a formal email.

03

Remember with permission

Corrections, preferences, and facts you explicitly approve. Inspect, correct, export, or delete them. Memories are notes, not hidden model training.

04

Know what is current

Headlines from Nigerian publishers with their names, links, and publication times. Cached stories are labelled with their real dates — never passed off as breaking news.

05

Teach without showing off

Explanations at your level, Nigerian examples when they help, practice questions, and honest uncertainty. Learning is a capability, not a costume.

06

Work on real phones

A small interface, no mandatory multi-gigabyte download to open the app, and model profiles that name their memory cost instead of promising magic.

A memory you can hold

It remembers. You decide what.

Memory lives in a local SQLite database, shared by Terminal Ara and Web Ara on this installation. Every note carries a kind, source, and confidence; memory cards name how the current search works. You can see what Ara is using — and remove it.

  • Recent corrections are surfaced so they carry more weight than older notes.
  • Lexical and local vector retrieval when configured, with the method labelled honestly.
  • Export and delete controls in the browser and the CLI.
  • No automatic retraining from your conversations.

Private by architecture

Your words stay where you put them.

No external AI API is required. Images and audio are sent only to the local endpoints you configure, and are not stored by Ara. Camera and microphone start only when you press their buttons, show a visible indicator, and stop when you say so.

Local modelOpenAI-compatible server on your machine or network.
Plain storageSQLite you can back up, inspect, and delete.
Honest limitsNo vision or voice claim without a configured engine.
Safe actionsNo shell or filesystem access from anonymous web users.

Bring your own model. Keep your own mind.

Ara is useful with a small local model, honest about what that model cannot do, and built to grow without changing its identity.
